Get started in 3 steps

1

Connect a phone service

A real number requires a supported number provider, account setup, and a secure backend. This starter does not purchase or assign numbers.

2

Choose where texts go

Apple Messages on iPad normally relays SMS/RCS from a compatible iPhone. A provider-issued number does not automatically appear in Messages.

3

Test messaging

After the service and routing are configured, test inbound and outbound texts before using the number.

⚠
Important limitation

iPadOS does not let an ordinary app add a new cellular phone number to Apple Messages. The real-number integration needs a compatible supported workflow; otherwise, texting must happen inside Poopywater's own messaging screen.
